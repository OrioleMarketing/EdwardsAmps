# Shop Filter and Reviews Validation

The development storefront was opened at the Shop anchor and the **Pedals** category control was selected. The Shop updated to show **5 products in effects pedals** and presented only the Effects Pedals group, confirming that the selected-category state and live result count work together.

The pedal detail pages use the new review section beneath their supplied product details. Its data source intentionally has no seeded entries, so the customer-facing empty state does not display ratings, quotations, customer names, or testimonials until verified and approved feedback is supplied.

Desktop and 390px mobile captures confirm that the review area follows the pedal controls without a blank panel, remains readable, and clearly communicates the verified-feedback requirement. The Shop filter controls remain available on mobile before the product results.
