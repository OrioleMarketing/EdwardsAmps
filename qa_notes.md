# Visual QA notes

The initial EdwardsAmps preview confirms that the dark workshop-editorial direction is rendering correctly. The hero section establishes strong mood, the typography feels premium, and the product imagery integrates well with the overall palette.

The main issue visible so far is proportional balance in the hero: on the current viewport, the headline sits very high and much of the large serif display text is obscured by the fold, which weakens the first-read impact. The lineup section reads well overall, but its opening heading is so large that it dominates the first card row and compresses the content relationship between introduction and products.

The mid-page sections remain visually coherent and the craftsmanship image reads strongly, but the large serif headings are currently too dominant on medium desktop widths. The section titles create impressive atmosphere, yet they occupy enough vertical space that adjacent explanatory copy and cards feel pushed downward. The next refinement should tighten heading scale and rebalance section spacing so the site keeps its editorial drama without sacrificing scanability.

The lower page confirms that the consultation and FAQ sections are structurally sound, but the right FAQ column feels visually underfilled when the accordion items are collapsed. The consultation cards also become slightly cramped at this width, with text lines stacking tightly inside the boxes. A final refinement should reduce the height imbalance between the consultation and FAQ columns and let the bottom section breathe more evenly.

The revised hero now presents the real Elusive Overdrive on a wooden workbench, which resolves the previous checkerboard issue and aligns the product presentation more closely with the workshop-editorial direction. The composition reads clearly, the product feels grounded, and the image supports the premium tone of the page without breaking contrast or hierarchy.
