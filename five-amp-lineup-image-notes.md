## Composition direction

The scene should show all five real Edwards amps arranged on a stage-like environment with dark flooring, subtle haze, low amber practical lighting, and restrained concert-style rim light. The image should read well inside a wide horizontal crop, with enough negative space around the group to avoid feeling cramped in the homepage layout.

The amps should not be evenly spaced like a catalog lineup. Instead, they should feel naturally staged for a backstage photo or quiet soundcheck moment, with slight variation in height and depth while remaining clearly visible as individual products.

The background should stay subdued and dark, avoiding performers, crowds, or distracting props. The focus is the five amps as a family portrait in a musical setting.

## Review notes

The composition is strongest as a wide supporting visual in the lineup intro because it preserves negative space and avoids feeling like another product card. It is ready either for direct user review or for insertion into the blank lineup intro area with a restrained frame treatment.
