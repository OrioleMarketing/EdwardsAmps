## Elusive Overdrive
- Positioning: flagship-style American-voiced amp with blackface-style cleans and overdrive that ranges from mild to wild.
- Format: offered as head, with hand-built cabinets available in house.
- Power options: 24W or 40W.
- Tubes: 6V6 or 6L6 platform depending on version described in source copy.
- Key selling points: wide gain range, classic control layout, smooth and punchy bass tones, hand-built turret board, custom chassis, custom Heyboer transformers.
- Source pricing seen on page: overview says base price starts at $3000 USD; specification list shows higher detailed pricing tiers for 24W/40W head and combo variants.

## King Richard
- Positioning: British-leaning model with sweet clean tones, overdrive, and jumpable/blendable channels.
- Format: head.
- Power: about 45W.
- Tubes: EL84 based, tube rectified, Class A.
- Channels: two, including one EF86 and one 12AX7 channel.
- Key selling points: chime, punch, authority, jumpable channels, hand-built board, custom chassis, custom Heyboer transformers.
- Source price on page: $2800 to $2900 USD for head depending on section.

## Hot Mama
- Positioning: compact British-inspired 15/18 watt style amp covering chiming cleans through overdrive without harshness.
- Formats: head or 1x12 combo.
- Power: about 18W.
- Tubes: EL84 based, tube rectified, Class A.
- Key selling points: interactive master volume, great recording amp, loud enough for live drums, portable, versatile for studio/church/stage.
- Source pricing: $1800 USD head, $2000 USD combo.

## Double Dee Tweed
- Positioning: tweed-inspired combo with more range than a typical 5E3, capable of chicken-pickin to Texas blues.
- Format: 1x12 combo.
- Power: about 18W.
- Tubes: 6V6 based, tube rectified, push-pull.
- Key selling points: tweed deluxe roots, more clean headroom and volume than expected, works for bedroom, studio, or stage, hand-built cabinet and components.
- Source pricing: $2000 USD combo.
